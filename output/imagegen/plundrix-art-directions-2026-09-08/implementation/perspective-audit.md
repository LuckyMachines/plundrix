# Perspective audit

Artifacts: `../05-recommended-synthesis.png` and `../../../../../app/reports/ui-review/latest/actual/instant-active-desktop.png`

Both the approved synthesis and production result use a straight-on, near-orthographic interface plane. Panel edges, rails, cards, and action plates remain parallel to the image axes. Object shading creates local thickness, but there are no reliable converging physical lines from which to estimate a finite vanishing point.

- Vanishing point: not estimated
- Normalized coordinates: not applicable
- Horizon assumption: effectively at infinity
- Fit residual: not applicable
- Perspective overlay: intentionally not generated

This is a deliberate geometry decision. Depth comes from overlap, relative scale, borders, inset treatment, and elevation rather than a camera projection that would distort responsive controls.
