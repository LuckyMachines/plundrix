# Plundrix poster prompt set

Generated with FLUX.2-pro through the existing Azure CLI helper. Final lettering and graphic framing were composed deterministically by `scripts/build-plundrix-posters.py`.

## Vertical art master

Use case: ads-marketing

Asset type: premium vertical key-art poster background for the strategy game Plundrix

Primary request: create a dramatic, playful vault-heist poster image that expresses a simultaneous race between PICK, SEARCH, and SABOTAGE without showing any words

Scene/backdrop: a cavernous retro-industrial vault chamber with one colossal circular safe door cracked open, sophisticated mechanical locking rings, brass and blackened steel, deep navy architectural shadows, four compact operator stations arranged like a competitive table

Subject: the vault door and a kinetic spread of clever heist gadgets - a precision lockpick, scanner lens, compact decoy relay, coiled sabotage cable, stolen keycard, scattered brass cogs - converging toward the vault; small mischievous evidence such as a clipped alarm wire and a tiny wheeled decoy escaping with a keycard

Style/medium: premium cinematic game key art fused with tactile 1970s espionage print design; realistic materials, strong graphic silhouette, subtle halftone grain and cream dossier-paper wedges, sophisticated rather than cartoonish

Composition/framing: 2:3 vertical poster, heroic low viewpoint; circular vault dominates the middle and upper-middle; gadgets create a triangular action path; leave a calm dark area across the top 22 percent for a large title and a clean dark footer area across the bottom 14 percent for campaign copy; important objects safely inside margins

Lighting/mood: tense but gleefully mischievous, warm tungsten light spilling from the vault, cyan scan light, muted green search signal, sharp vermilion sabotage spark; high contrast and legible at thumbnail size

Color palette: graphite black, deep blue-black, aged bronze, warm cream paper, cyan, muted oxide green, vermilion red

Materials/textures: worn steel, oily brass, rubber cable, scratched enamel, technical paper, restrained film grain

Constraints: no text, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no watermark, no skulls, no pirate imagery, no people or faces, no guns, no cryptocurrency symbols; preserve generous clean title space; make it look like a distinctive strategy game rather than a generic shooter

Avoid: purple neon cyberpunk, fantasy runes, bank-lobby realism, cluttered UI overlays, generic sci-fi corridors, excessive sparks, centered character portrait
