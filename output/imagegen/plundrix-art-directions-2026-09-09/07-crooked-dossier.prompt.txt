Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity desktop web-game art-direction study for Plundrix Career
Primary request: Redesign the career screen as an operator dossier that feels accumulated, personal, and sly instead of a grid of empty analytics cards. The player should feel that every run leaves physical evidence and a funny little scar.
Scene/backdrop: The interface fills the image edge to edge. A deep charcoal operations archive holds one oversized open dossier folder assembled from blueprint leaves, stamped reports, clipped photos, route traces, inventory tags, and small precision hardware. This is an interface, not a photographed desk.
Subject: A hero operator identity plate at upper left, an expressive crooked rank stamp, a winding progression trail made of mission stamps and red thread, a rival ledger that looks annotated rather than tabular, a device-mastery drawer with several gadget silhouettes, and a prominent next-operation ticket. Zero-state information should still look inviting and intentional rather than empty.
Style/medium: Premium shippable game UI; 60 percent impossible blueprint, 25 percent punch-card caper, 15 percent cut-paper dossier, with brass reserved for clips, seals, trophies, and gadget hardware. Graphic, tactile, playful, legible, and not nostalgic cosplay.
Composition/framing: Straight-on near-orthographic desktop UI, 1536 by 1024. Use one dominant dossier composition rather than many same-sized rectangles. Strong visual path from identity to next objective to progression to rivals to mastery. Large touch targets and clear reading order.
Lighting/mood: Quiet after-hours archive with warm paper pools against midnight blue, mint success stamps, coral grudges, and a small warm spotlight on the next-operation ticket. The mood is clever self-mythology with a wink.
Color palette: Ink black, midnight blue, faded blueprint, warm bone paper, oxidized brass, traffic orange, mint, and vermilion stamp ink.
Materials/textures: Toothful paper, folded tabs, staples, blackened metal, woven thread, rubber-stamp ink, scratched photo emulsion, tiny embossed seals.
Text (verbatim): "PLUNDRIX", "OPERATOR FILE", and "NEXT OPERATION" only. Keep every other copy area blank, ruled, stamped with abstract marks, or represented by simple bars so live data remains code-rendered.
Constraints: Full-bleed UI only; no device frame; the career state must remain usable at zero progress and rich at high progress; show hierarchy through physical grouping, scale, and color rather than equal cards; keep room for accessible real text; create repeatable components that could be built with CSS plus reusable raster textures.
Avoid: Generic achievement dashboard, corporate analytics, empty black voids, fantasy parchment, steampunk overload, detective cliche clutter, photoreal hands, fake paragraphs, tiny illegible labels, glossy casino surfaces, excessive gradients, watermark.
