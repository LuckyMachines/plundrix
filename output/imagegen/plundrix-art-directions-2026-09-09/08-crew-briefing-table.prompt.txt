Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity desktop web-game art-direction study for Plundrix live multiplayer lobby
Primary request: Turn the live lobby into a social heist-table ritual. Make joining, seat readiness, wallet state, and launch progress instantly understandable while giving each crew member a vivid presence before the match starts.
Scene/backdrop: The interface fills the image edge to edge. A dark blueprint briefing table is viewed straight on, with four physical crew-seat plates arranged around a central operation plan. Cut-paper invitation slips, readiness seals, route string, and a compact launch mechanism create tactile depth without becoming a literal tabletop photograph.
Subject: Four clearly differentiated crew seats with strong mask silhouettes and color identities, two occupied and two open; a central mission blueprint with a visible start-to-vault route; a large invitation ticket; a chunky connect-or-join control; a four-step launch rail that physically advances; and a small honest Sepolia beta notice integrated as a stamped footer area. The lobby should visually answer who is here, what happens next, and who can act.
Style/medium: Premium shippable multiplayer game UI; impossible blueprint structure plus screen-printed punch-card energy and modest cut-paper charm. Social, conspiratorial, mischievous, inclusive, and highly legible. Hardware is functional and scarce.
Composition/framing: Straight-on near-orthographic desktop UI, 1536 by 1024. Central plan and crew ring are the dominant mass. Primary join action is unmistakable. Occupied, ready, waiting, and unavailable states differ by shape, icon, brightness, and pattern, not color alone.
Lighting/mood: A secret midnight briefing with cyan plan light, warm paper edges, mint ready signals, coral risk marks, and a tiny brass launch latch. Feels like friends leaning into a caper, without showing people or hands.
Color palette: Ink black, midnight navy, cobalt blueprint, chalk cream, oxidized brass, cyan, mint, vermilion, and one warm amber action color.
Materials/textures: Blueprint cloth, paper tickets, screen-print ink, enamel seat tokens, black steel rails, thread, clips, subtle stamped fingerprints.
Text (verbatim): "PLUNDRIX", "CREW BRIEFING", "JOIN CREW", and "SEPOLIA BETA" only. Keep names and all other labels as clean blank fields or abstract bars for code-rendered data.
Constraints: Full-bleed UI only; no monitor or browser frame; preserve clear accessibility and large targets; represent state redundantly; each seat must feel personable without relying on character portraits; make the flow from connect to join to ready to launch visually continuous; components must plausibly translate into HTML and CSS.
Avoid: Generic SaaS lobby, Discord clone, esports broadcast chrome, neon cyberpunk, casino styling, dense chat, photoreal people, tiny wallet strings, fake paragraphs, holograms, excessive gradients, ornamental gears, watermark.
