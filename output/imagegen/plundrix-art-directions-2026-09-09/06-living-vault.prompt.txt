Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity desktop web-game art-direction study for Plundrix Vault Run
Primary request: Design a shippable, front-on 16:10 game interface where the vault itself is the visual thesis. Turn the current sparse route-selection dashboard into a living lockwork machine that makes choosing a route feel like touching a mischievous heist board.
Scene/backdrop: The interface fills the image edge to edge. A midnight-blue architectural blueprint and dark evidence-board surface sit beneath layered paper tickets, inset metal rails, screws, string routes, and a large functional circular vault lock. This is an interface, not a photograph of a monitor or desk.
Subject: A large central vault mechanism occupying roughly 45 percent of the canvas, with five visibly connected lock stages across the top and three route choices physically branching from the mechanism. Each route is a large distinct ticket or hinged plate with a readable risk silhouette: reckless is a kicked hinge, cunning is a bribed map, careful is a rumor trail. A compact rival dossier and heat/lives/score strip remain visible without competing with the vault.
Style/medium: Premium shippable game UI; an impossible-blueprint caper mixed with screen-printed evidence folders and restrained precision hardware. Clever, tactile, sly, handmade, and legible. Use crisp silhouettes, imperfect ink, paper fibers, engraved metal, subtle edge wear, and real contact shadows. Brass is reserved for mechanisms and rewards, never used as a blanket skin.
Composition/framing: Straight-on near-orthographic desktop UI, 1536 by 1024. Strong center of gravity. Clear top status rail, dominant middle mechanism, large bottom decision plates, compact side intelligence panel. Large click targets and calm negative space.
Lighting/mood: Low-key museum-after-hours lighting with soft cyan technical glow inside the active lock, warm brass glints on moving parts, and small coral warning accents. Mischievous rather than grim.
Color palette: Ink black, midnight navy, blueprint cobalt, chalk cream, oxidized brass, cyan route light, restrained vermilion and mint signals.
Materials/textures: Blueprint paper, cut dossier stock, blackened steel, worn brass, silk-screen ink, thread, rivets, glass gauge covers.
Text (verbatim): "PLUNDRIX" and "VAULT RUN" only. Keep every other text area as clean blank label wells or simple geometric marks so real UI copy can be rendered in code.
Constraints: Full-bleed UI only; no device frame; preserve excellent information hierarchy; the mechanism must look interactive rather than decorative; all routes must visibly connect to the vault; provide obvious code-renderable label areas; use a consistent design-system grammar that could extend to Career and live multiplayer.
Avoid: Generic flat dashboard cards, empty black voids, neon cyberpunk, casino luxury, steampunk clutter, photoreal hands, tiny illegible text, fake paragraphs, floating holograms, excessive gradients, ornamental gears with no function, logos other than the requested wordmark, watermark.
