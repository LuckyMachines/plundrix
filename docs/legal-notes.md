# Plundrix Legal Notes

Current intended product posture:

- Sepolia staging is live
- mainnet is not live yet
- the public beta offers FREE mode only: no entry fees and no prizes
- STAKES code and fee configuration exist in the contract but are dormant product capabilities
- the public app hides STAKES mode unless an operator deliberately enables `VITE_ENABLE_STAKES=true`
- any paid competition launch requires a separate product decision, jurisdiction-specific legal review, updated terms, and updated user-facing disclosures
- player decisions affect outcome probabilities, but PICK and SEARCH also use variable onchain resolution; do not claim that outcomes contain no chance

User-facing claims should stay aligned with this posture:

- do not describe the game as gambling, sweepstakes, or lottery
- do not market or describe STAKES mode as available while it remains disabled
- describe the actual mechanics accurately: strategy changes probabilities and table position; some action outcomes are variable
- do not market fee testing on Sepolia as a live economy
- emphasize that skill (action selection, timing, tool management) determines outcomes

Operational notes:

- players may still pay normal blockchain gas fees for on-chain transactions
- smart contract interactions remain subject to normal wallet, network, and transaction risks
- agent and bot participation should be disclosed clearly where relevant
- the marketing site should expose public Terms and Privacy pages
- Privacy copy should reflect actual site behavior, including the self-hosted Plausible analytics script currently enabled in `app/index.html`

This file is a product-claims reference for the repo. It is not legal advice.
