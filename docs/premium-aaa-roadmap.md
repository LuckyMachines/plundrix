# Plundrix Premium Presentation Roadmap

This roadmap defines "AAA feel" as coherence, authored response, sensory clarity, and emotional payoff - not a larger feature count or an expensive real-time renderer.

## v0.1 baseline - 2026-09-13

Overall presentation grade: **B**

| Area | Grade | Evidence and A gap |
|---|---:|---|
| Art direction | A- | Nightfall Vault has a distinct industrial-heist identity. It still needs restrained wear, operational detail, and asymmetry to feel inhabited. |
| Decision clarity | A- | Pick, Search, and Sabotage are readable and responsive. Resolution and the next decision still compete visually. |
| Round theater | C+ | Outcomes update correctly, but anticipation, reveal, impact, and recovery are not yet staged as one authored sequence. |
| Sound direction | C | Functional synthesized cues exist, but there is no reusable layered audio director or action-specific mechanical signature. |
| Motion and game feel | B- | Useful transitions and reduced-motion support exist. Motion lacks a shared anticipation-impact-recovery grammar. |
| Rival personality | B- | Rivals have names, strategies, colors, and outcomes. Their stations do not yet react with distinctive physical character. |
| Gadget embodiment | B | Gadget art and signature moments are strong UI objects. They need physical entry, activation, and recovery within the world. |
| Progression in the world | C+ | Lock progress is visible. Persistent scars, pressure, and earned environmental change are limited. |
| Production discipline | A | Deterministic captures, world builds, budgets, accessibility, and release checks are already automated. |

## A bar, stricter

- A complete round reads as anticipation, sealed commitment, simultaneous reveal, physical consequence, and controlled recovery.
- Pick, Search, and Sabotage are identifiable from motion and sound before reading their labels.
- The resolved action remains visually separate from the next selected plan.
- Rival reactions show who acted, who was affected, and whether the outcome succeeded.
- Gadgets visibly enter, activate, and leave the world without obstructing controls.
- Lock progress changes the room through light, wear, pressure, and breach preparation.
- Motion, audio, haptics, contrast, and reduced-motion behavior share one authored state contract.
- Desktop, laptop, and mobile preserve the same focal sequence.
- The complete effect remains lightweight enough for a solo developer to maintain.

## Evidence status

Automated process quality is A. Human evidence remains intentionally unclaimed: first-time comprehension, perceived impact, replay desire, and audio delight still require observed sessions with real players.

## Solo-developer rule

Polish one vertical slice first: choose action -> commit -> reveal -> consequence -> next decision. Reuse that grammar everywhere. Do not add a heavy real-time runtime unless player evidence proves that layered rendering cannot deliver the required interaction.
